<p class="partners-intro">The Business AI and Analytics Centre (BAIAC) works with academic institutions, technology partners, professional communities, and public-sector organisations to advance research, education, and innovation in artificial intelligence and analytics.</p>

<div class="baiac-grid partners-grid">
  <article class="baiac-card partner-card">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">Academic collaboration</p>
    <h2>Durham Rutgers Accounting Analytics Network (DRAAN)</h2>
    <p>The Durham Rutgers Accounting Analytics Network (DRAAN) is an international collaboration between Durham University Business School and Rutgers Business School.</p>
    <p>DRAAN supports research, training, conferences, and collaboration in accounting analytics, auditing, artificial intelligence, reporting technologies, and digital transformation.</p>
    <a class="baiac-button baiac-button--solid" href="https://www.durham.ac.uk/business/about/departments/accounting/durham-rutgers-accounting-analytics-network-draan/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learn More <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
  </article>

  <article class="baiac-card partner-card">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">Technology partnership</p>
    <h2>Microsoft AI Skills Centre of Excellence</h2>
    <p>The AI Skills Centre of Excellence is a Durham University initiative developed in partnership with Microsoft.</p>
    <p>The Centre supports students, staff, businesses, and public-sector organisations in building practical AI skills and understanding how AI can enhance research, teaching, and organisational effectiveness.</p>
    <a class="baiac-button baiac-button--solid" href="https://www.durham.ac.uk/news-events/latest-news/2026/06/durham-and-microsoft-partnership-to-support-ai-skills-and-future-opportunities-/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learn More <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
  </article>

  <article class="baiac-card partner-card">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">UK research initiative</p>
    <h2>INFORMED-AI Hub</h2>
    <p>INFORMED-AI is a major UK research initiative bringing together universities, researchers, and partners to investigate how artificial intelligence can support better decision making in complex real-world systems.</p>
    <p>The Hub develops new methods, tools, and applications at the intersection of AI, operations research, optimisation, and human decision making.</p>
