The Centre studies how artificial intelligence and analytics are changing organisations, markets and professional practice. Its research brings together perspectives from across Durham University Business School and connects technical, organisational and societal questions.

The themes below are connected areas of enquiry, not separate departments. Example member affiliations indicate the breadth of expertise represented across the Centre; individual interests may span several themes.

<div class="baiac-grid baiac-grid--themes baiac-grid--page">

    <p class="baiac-card__label">01 · Decisions and operations</p>
    <h2>Decision Intelligence and Optimisation</h2>
    <p>Research on how data, models and computational methods can support better decisions in complex organisations.</p>
    <p><strong>Example topics:</strong> planning, forecasting, resource allocation, operations research, optimisation and decision support.</p>
    <p><strong>Example member affiliations:</strong> Operations Research (Neil Walton); Operational Research (Nalan Gulpinar); Operations Management (Fang Liu).</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="accounting-audit-assurance-analytics">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">02 · Accountability and reporting</p>
    <h2>Accounting, Audit and Assurance Analytics</h2>
    <p>Research on how analytics and digital tools affect accounting practice, assurance and organisational accountability.</p>
    <p><strong>Example topics:</strong> audit analytics, continuous auditing, governance, accounting information systems, forensic analytics, ESG assurance and digital reporting.</p>
    <p><strong>Example member affiliations:</strong> Accounting (Amir Michael, Kathy D. Wei, Evans Boamah, Guanming He and Terry A. Harris).</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="digital-organisations-information-systems">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">03 · Technology and organisations</p>
    <h2>Digital Organisations and Information Systems</h2>
    <p>Research on how technologies are adopted and how they reshape organisational structures, work and change.</p>
    <p><strong>Example topics:</strong> technology adoption, enterprise systems, digital transformation and organisational change.</p>
    <p><strong>Example member affiliations:</strong> Business and Computing (Mariann Hardey); Strategy and Innovation (Bettina Becker); Strategy (Xinwei Shi).</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="markets-consumers-behaviour">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">04 · Markets and behaviour</p>
    <h2>Markets, Consumers and Behaviour</h2>
    <p>Research on how data and digital technologies influence markets, consumer choices and interactions.</p>
    <p><strong>Example topics:</strong> marketing analytics, experimentation, consumer behaviour and digital markets.</p>
    <p><strong>Example member affiliations:</strong> Marketing (Arezou Ghiassaleh and Xinming He).</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="finance-risk-economic-analytics">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">05 · Finance and economic systems</p>
    <h2>Finance, Risk and Economic Analytics</h2>
    <p>Research on analytical approaches to financial systems, economic questions and uncertainty.</p>
    <p><strong>Example topics:</strong> banking, economic modelling, fintech, financial systems and risk management.</p>
    <p><strong>Example member affiliations:</strong> Finance and Economics (Kevin Dowd); Accounting and Finance (Julian M. Williams); Finance (Baback Roodbar).</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="responsible-ai-societal-impact">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">06 · Governance and society</p>
    <h2>Responsible AI and Societal Impact</h2>
    <p>Research on the responsibilities and wider consequences that accompany the adoption of AI and analytics.</p>
    <p><strong>Example topics:</strong> ethics, governance, regulation, transparency, sustainability and public-sector applications.</p>
    <p><strong>Example member affiliations:</strong> Business and Computing and the Leverhulme Centre for Algorithmic Life (Mariann Hardey); management and research leadership (Olga Epitropaki).</p>
