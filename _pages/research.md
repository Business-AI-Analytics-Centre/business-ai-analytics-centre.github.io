We study how computational methods and digital technologies are developed, adopted and used in organisations and society. Our research spans the design of methods, their application to business questions and the consequences of data-informed decisions.

The themes below describe areas of interest across the Centre. They are not intended to be an exhaustive list of individual projects.

    <h2>Artificial Intelligence</h2>
    <p>Research on AI systems and their use in business, including adoption, organisational change, accountability and effects on work and decision-making.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="analytics">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">02 · Evidence and inference</p>
    <h2>Analytics</h2>
    <p>Methods for extracting, interpreting and communicating insight from data, and the conditions under which analytical evidence informs practice.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="operations-research">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">03 · Systems and decisions</p>
    <h2>Operations Research</h2>
    <p>Mathematical modelling and computational techniques for understanding and improving complex operational and strategic systems.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="optimisation">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">04 · Choice under constraints</p>
    <h2>Optimisation</h2>
    <p>Models and algorithms for allocating resources and comparing choices when objectives, constraints and uncertainty interact.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="baiac-card baiac-theme" id="information-systems">
    <p class="baiac-card__label">05 · Digital work and institutions</p>
    <h2>Information Systems</h2>
    <p>Research on digital technologies in organisations, including their design, use and influence on work practices and relationships.</p>
