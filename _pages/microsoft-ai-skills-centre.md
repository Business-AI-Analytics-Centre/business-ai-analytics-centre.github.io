The Microsoft AI Skills Centre of Excellence is a Durham University and Microsoft partnership focused on developing AI skills and future opportunities.

The initiative brings together expertise, tools and support to help students, staff, graduates, businesses and the wider public develop practical, responsible and inclusive AI skills. It also aims to strengthen how AI informs teaching, research and working practices.

This work complements the Business AI and Analytics Centre's interest in connecting research with the ways organisations and communities understand and use AI.
