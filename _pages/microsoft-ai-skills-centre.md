The Microsoft AI Skills Centre of Excellence is a Durham University and Microsoft partnership focused on building AI capability and supporting future opportunities.

The partnership supports AI skills development for staff and students, and brings expertise, tools and support to help people develop practical, responsible and inclusive capabilities. It also aims to help organisations consider how AI may transform their work, teaching, research and services.

Regional engagement is part of the initiative's wider ambition, connecting the University with businesses, public-sector organisations and communities. This work complements the Business AI and Analytics Centre's interest in how AI and analytics are changing organisations and society.
