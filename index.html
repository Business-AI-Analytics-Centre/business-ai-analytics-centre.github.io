01 About the Centre

Turning data into insight, and insight into impact.

The Business AI and Analytics Centre (BAIAC) is a research community at Durham University Business School. We explore how artificial intelligence and analytics are changing organisations, markets and society.

By connecting interdisciplinary scholarship with business and policy, we help shape practical, thoughtful approaches to the opportunities and challenges of a data-rich world.
