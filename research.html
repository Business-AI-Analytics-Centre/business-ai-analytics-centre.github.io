What we study

Research with

We examine how AI and analytics are reshaping business — and how organisations can respond with purpose.

01 / RESEARCH
01 Our approach

Questions that matter
for business and society.

Our research spans the development, adoption and impact of AI and analytics. We draw on the strengths of business scholarship and interdisciplinary collaboration to understand not only what these technologies can do, but how they are used and who they affect.

Research themes evolve with the questions being explored across our community. The areas below offer a guide to the Centre's interests.

01

Organisations & work

AI in organisations

How do organisations adopt and govern AI? We are interested in the effects on strategy, work, leadership and organisational change.

↗
02

Methods & decisions

Analytics and decision-making

Exploring how data, models and human judgement combine in decisions — and what makes analytical insight useful and trustworthy.

↗
03

Responsibility & society

Responsible and inclusive AI

Examining accountability, fairness, transparency and the societal implications of AI-enabled business activity.

↗
04

Innovation & value

Data-driven business

Understanding how data and intelligent technologies shape innovation, business models, customer experience and value creation.
