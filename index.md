      <div class="section-label"><span>01</span> Our Focus</div>
      <h2 id="focus-heading">Exploring the future of organisations</h2>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="intro-content">
    <div class="intro-text">
      <p>The Business AI and Analytics Centre explores how organisations can use artificial intelligence and analytics to create value, improve decision making, redesign processes and develop new capabilities.</p>
      <p>Our members study how these technologies are transforming work, markets, governance, financial systems, accounting practices and business operations.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="intro-text">
      <p>We are interested not only in what AI can do, but in what future organisations should look like when these capabilities are widely available.</p>
      <p>BAIAC is a Business School-wide research community exploring the future of organisations in the age of AI and analytics.</p>
